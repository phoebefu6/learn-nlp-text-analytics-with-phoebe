Learn NLP & Text Analytics with Phoebe

Text as data, before and without a large language model. Tokenise, weight, classify and search real complaint text in your browser, and find out when a two-line classifier beats the model everyone wants to buy. Every session asks one question: what can be counted, classified and searched cheaply, auditably and reproducibly, and where does a big model actually earn its cost?

The six sessions 🎯 · from a pile of text to a signed build-or-buy note

The running case is Ledgerly, a constructed consumer-lending fintech whose complaints team must route every complaint to one of six product queues and spot rising issues early. The team is fictional; the complaints are real: 3,000 narratives US consumers sent to the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau in 2023 and agreed to publish. Each session adds one page to Ledgerly's text playbook.

📄 Session 1 · foundations

Text is a table you have not built yet

Documents, tokens and a vocabulary; what case folding, stop words, stemming and redaction marks each throw away; Zipf's law in 3,000 real complaints. Build-along: Ledgerly's corpus card.

▶ Start here ⚖️ Session 2 · core

Weight the words

Counts against TF-IDF against bigrams; sparse vectors; how big a vocabulary needs to be. Build-along: the top words of all six product queues under three weightings.

counts, TF-IDF, n-grams 🧭 Session 3 · core

Route it: a classifier you can read

Naive Bayes and logistic regression on the word table; reading a model's weights; precision and recall per queue; the confusion table as a routing cost. Build-along: Ledgerly's routing classifier and its error table.

readable, not magic 🔬 Session 4 · bench night

The text bench

Six rungs from majority class to a tuned logistic model on 600 held-out complaints, computed in your browser; a training-rows trap, a shuffled-labels break button, and a modelled LLM rung priced, not pretended.

★ the bench 🔎 Session 5 · stretch

Search and themes

BM25 against cosine search over the corpus; k-means themes that change when you change the seed; a theme that more than quadruples between June and October 2023. Build-along: Ledgerly's theme watchlist.

find it, then watch it 🏁 Session 6 · capstone

When the big model earns its place

Cost per thousand documents, latency, audit and reproducibility against accuracy; the published disagreement on zero-shot LLM labelling against trained classifiers. Capstone: Ledgerly's signed build-or-buy note.

What is honest here 🧾

The bench computes for real; the running case is constructed and labelled; methods are read at their source; contested evidence is taught as a disagreement.
